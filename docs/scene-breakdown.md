# Scene breakdown

Shared specification for every scene: straight-on or high-angle camera, no handheld motion; night stage in charcoal with one drifting pool of blue light; warm crowd; mono numerals; Hindi captions low-centre inside Instagram's safe area; 0.36 s cross-fades. Must not appear: real people, party symbols, flags, readable third-party logos, any number without its unit.
